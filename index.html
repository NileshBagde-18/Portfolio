About Me

Short Summary

As a Product Designer , I design and deliver intuitive and engaging user interfaces for a B2B SaaS platform that helps sales teams close more deals faster.

I collaborate with designers, product managers, engineers, stakeholders and customers to understand their needs and pain points, and to test and iterate on my design solutions.
